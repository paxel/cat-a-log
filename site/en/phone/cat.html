Everything about one cat: photos, fields, family, what is planned and its history. The page is read-only until you tap the pen.

What you see

The photos first, the star on the profile image. Then what is planned for the cat and the clowder it lives in, then the fields with their current values. A hold on a field jumps into editing it; a hold on a photo opens its menu: profile image, crop, mark, share, remove. The clock on a field opens its history, a number's history is a graph.

Editing

The pen turns on editing: every field gets its editor, a new field is added at the bottom, the name is changed by a tap on the title. Private is set in a field's editor; a private value stays on this device unless a share says otherwise. See Weigh a cat for a measurement.

The bar

Help, the card, the pen, the timeline, the vet report, the missing poster, then as far as they fit: move to another catalog, seen here now, flier hangs here, add flier, share publicly, hide, merge into, delete. The rest sits in ⋮; turn the phone and the menu empties.
