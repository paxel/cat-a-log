The overview of your clowders, the places cats live: your home, a foster home, a shelter. The name at the top is the catalog you are in.

What you see

One card per clowder with its cover picture, its faces and its status; a star marks a favourite, which sorts first. The strays card collects every cat that has no home right now. A tap on a card opens the clowder, a hold opens its menu with the cat ear. The button at the bottom right makes a new clowder.

The bar

Help, the view switch between cards and a table, search across every cat, the agenda, the map and sync are on the bar; what does not fit sits in the ⋮ menu: show hidden things, find duplicates, open conflicts while there are some, achievements, settings, about. A wider screen puts more of them on the bar.

The catalog name

A tap on the name at the top switches to another catalog or makes a new one. Everything in a catalog stays in it; see Catalogs.
