Two rules carry the phone: a tap is the primary action, a hold the secondary one. The desk answers to keys and the right-click.

On the phone

GestureWhat it does
TapOpens the thing; on a box, ticks it. A tap never opens a menu.
HoldThe menu, or the editor when there is only one thing to do. Everything with a hold wears the cat ear in its corner.
Swipe left on a noteThe next note.
Swipe right on a noteClears the news; failures stay.
Hold on the mapRecords a sighting or a flier for a chosen cat.
Hold on a pin or a trail dotIts menu: history, correction, removal, flier taken down.
Pinch and drag on the posterFrames a photo.

Nothing slides in from the bottom; every menu opens where the finger is. A single choice of many is a list of radio buttons, never a row of pills.

On the desk

KeyWhat it does
Ctrl 1 to Ctrl 7Home, Cats, Clowders, Map, Agenda, Vet, Family.
Arrows, EnterWalk a table, open the marked row as a card.
Ctrl click, Shift clickMark several rows.
EscapeCloses the modal on top.
Right-clickThe same menu as the ⋮ beside a row, a card, a pin or a photo.
Shift drag on the mapA box to zoom into.
Drag a card's title bar, its bottom edgeMoves it, resizes it.
