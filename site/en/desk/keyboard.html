The desk is usable without the mouse. These are the keys it answers to.

KeyWhat it does
Ctrl 1 to Ctrl 7Home, Cats, Clowders, Map, Agenda, Vet, Family
ArrowsWalk the rows of a table
EnterOpen the marked row as a card on the desk
Ctrl click, Shift clickMark several rows, one more or a range
EscapeClose the modal on top, or the menu
Shift drag on the mapDraw a box and zoom into it
Type in a tableGoes to the search box

Right-click on a row, a card, a pin or a photo opens the same menu the ⋮ beside it does.
